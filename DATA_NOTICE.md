# Data notice

The files in `data/` contain no images. GDXray+ may be used free of charge for research and educational purposes only, and its redistribution and commercial use are prohibited, so the radiographs and the casting patch images are not included: download GDXray+ from its authors and regenerate the patches with `scripts/make_casting_patches.py`.

The tables in `data/` are derived from:

* **GDXray+** (D. Mery et al., *Journal of Nondestructive Evaluation* 34, 42, 2015). Cite it when you use these files.
* **BAM round-robin weld radiographs** (GDXray+ series W0003). The W0003 section labels are derived from BAM's `real-values.xls`. BAM provides the images and annotations free of charge for scientific use and prohibits commercial use; please acknowledge BAM Berlin (Federal Institute for Materials Research and Testing) when publishing work that uses them.

The section labels, film index, casting patch index and split definitions are released for non-commercial research use under the same conditions.
